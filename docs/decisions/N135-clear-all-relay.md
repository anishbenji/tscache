# N135: Whether `clearAll` reaches other workers

Row N135 of the decision table in `docs/architecture.md` §8, whose introduction says how its rows were taken. Recorded in step 135 from the M5 design proposal (`docs/design/m5-deploy-skew.md`), which keeps the alternatives in full. Decisions are locked (`AGENTS.md`); the architecture section lists every decision with a one-line summary.

## Resolution

**Yes, by a relay on the workers' channel.** Every `clearAll` carries a clear id, chosen where the call was made. The originator posts `{ t: 'clearAll', v: 1, id, clear }` twice: in the synchronous step that clears its memory, and when its own disk clear has settled, whether it succeeded, failed or reached its deadline. A SharedWorker that receives a message from another worker runs `clearAll` as if one of its own tabs had called it, for each message it receives. A worker that clears for a clear id it did not originate posts once, with that id, when its first disk clear for that id settles, and never again for that id; it keeps a small set of the ids it has seen. The dedicated-worker and in-process hostings post too, opening the channel at the call and closing it after their second post. The calling tab's promise does not wait for the other workers. The relay closes the logout gap N54 and N66 record for another SharedWorker, a worker that starts while the clear is pending, a lagging writer and chains of late workers included (proven by contract test 10); a failed clear, workers of 0.1.0 and 0.2.0, a realm without `BroadcastChannel` and a hosting without IndexedDB remain. Cost: with k other workers each worker clears about k + 1 times per call. This extends the reach N54 and N66 give `clearAll`; the user approved their amendment and §4.10.7's explicitly. Rejected: two posts without a relay (narrows the gap but does not close it: a worker that flushed before handling the posts leaves records a later worker loads and keeps); only from SharedWorkers (leaves the dedicated and in-process gap); not at all. User decision 71, row F11 option (b), 2026-11-16 (§2.7, §4.10.7, §4.10.8, step 135)

## Amendment, 2026-11-21

User decision 2026-11-21 (#120): the set of seen clear ids is kept for the worker's life. "A small set" above is small in practice, one id per `clearAll` the worker hears, but it is not bounded: a bound by count let the clears circulate among workers for their lives (gate round 1, R1-1, `docs/reviews/step-135.md`), so a worker never forgets an id it has seen. The relay, its message and its two posts are unchanged.
