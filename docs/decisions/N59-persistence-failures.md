# N59: Persistence failures

Row N59 of the decision table in `docs/architecture.md` §8, whose introduction says how its rows were taken. The text below is that row, moved here unchanged in step 73. Decisions are locked (`AGENTS.md`); the architecture section lists every decision with a one-line summary.

## Resolution

**Never a rejected call.** The store failing to open, or a load beyond 5 s, stops the cache with `'unavailable'`; a failed read with `'error'`; a quota error stops it at once (N47). A failed write is retried after a second with the union of the dirty state, until the job's deadline (N61): then the cache stops, with `'error'` and the last error when an attempt was rejected, or `'unavailable'` when none settled. A stopped cache's records are deleted with one strict write under its own deadline, since the disk may cover what memory withdrew; when that deletion fails as well, the records stay, the one way a removal can outlive its call. Rejected: retrying forever (a silent failure); stopping on the first transient failure. Design, 2026-10-30; user-confirmed 2026-10-30. **Changed in substance after review round 2:** the first design bounded each store call at 5 s, retried three times 5 s apart and gave lock acquisition no bound, so the "bounded" wait of a removal was the sum of an unbounded number of bounded parts (round 2, R2-2). Now one deadline per job of the writer, `timeoutMs` (5 s) from the moment it is due, covers queue, lock, store calls and retries together; the retry count and the per-call timeout are gone. The 5 s bound on `cache()` (N46) is the same option. Redesign, 2026-10-30. **Clarified 2026-10-31 (step 38, lazy loading, §4.10.8):** in the SharedWorker the persister is a chunk loaded at the first persisted open or `clearAll`; a chunk that fails to load, or does not load within the load's 5 s, stops every waiting and later open with `'unavailable'` and turns persistence off for the worker's life, as a store that fails to open does.
