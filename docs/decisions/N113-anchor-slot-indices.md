# N113: What a reader checks when its zone data may differ from the writer's (G8)

Row N113 of the decision table in `docs/architecture.md` §8, whose introduction says how its rows were taken. Recorded in step 129 from the calendar grid design proposal (`docs/design/calendar-grid.md`, row G8), which keeps the alternatives in full. Decisions are locked (`AGENTS.md`); the architecture section lists every decision with a one-line summary.

## Resolution

**The slot index of each anchor, within a budget of offset lookups.** A format 2 snapshot or page record (N114) stores, beside each instant that names a slot, the slot index its writer gave it: `startSlot` beside each payload's `start`, and `rangeSlots` and `coverageSlots` beside the envelope's `range` and `coverage`. A reader requires every such instant to be a slot start, as before, and then to be the start of that slot; otherwise it refuses the record as it refuses a malformed one. A record that applies therefore has its coverage and points in the writer's slots, so every bar is on the local date, week or month its writer put it on, and a move across the date line that one runtime lacks always refuses. What stays open, by this decision: a difference at a slot the record names that is no anchor's. A daylight-saving rule one runtime lacks applies silently when both ends of the record fall in the agreeing season, and its bars then read at the reader's start of their slots, which is what a fetch of its own would give the reader too. The work is bounded before it starts: a record's charge is 24 for its range, 36 for each coverage range, 12 for each payload and 12 for a watermark, computed from its shape with no lookup, and `hydrate` refuses a snapshot whose charge exceeds the budget of 524,288 lookups, while a load applies the newest pages within it and skips the others (§4.10.5). Writers have no budget. This amends the validation of `hydrate` (§4.5, N35) and of the load (§4.10.5, N57), with the user's explicit approval. Rejected: nothing new (a whole-day difference at an anchor moves points one session and covers a slot nobody fetched); sampled slot starts (misses single days, and a refusal is a record fetched again to the same instants); the start of every named slot, as runs or as a digest (exact, but a load then takes time in proportion to covered slots, with a canonical form, a larger cost model and more amendments); a stamp of the zone data's version (no runtime exposes one where records are read); slot indices in place of instants (gives up the refusal where refusing is right). User decision 83, row G8 option (c), 2026-11-21 (§2.8, §4.5, §4.10.5, step 129)
