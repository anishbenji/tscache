# N52: Which hostings persist (P8; first "One engine per tab")

Row N52 of the decision table in `docs/architecture.md` §8, whose introduction says how its rows were taken. The text below is that row, moved here unchanged in step 73. Decisions are locked (`AGENTS.md`); the architecture section lists every decision with a one-line summary.

## Resolution

**Only the SharedWorker hosting, where one engine owns the database of its origin and writes it plainly from memory, with no lock (§4.10.7). In the dedicated-worker and in-process hostings there is no persister and `persist: true` does nothing (N45); since 2026-11-02 their `clearAll` empties the database with a clear-only call (N66).** Two SharedWorkers overlap only briefly (a deploy skew; a closing worker and its successor); page records are self-contained (N55) and only the cache record moves the watermark, so the overlap costs coverage, and at worst one engine writes back a range the other removed while it still holds it. User decision #8, 2026-10-31 (Option A of `pending-user-decisions.md`), after four design rounds whose last two found every issue in the several-engine path (`docs/reviews/step-38.md`). Rejected: keeping the first resolution below (four rounds did not converge); parking persistence entirely (loses the main reload win). Cost accepted: Chrome for Android before 148 and pinned dedicated modes get no persistence until the several-engine case is designed again (§4.10.10). **The first resolution, retired 2026-10-31 (user decision #8: SharedWorker only):** dedicated and in-process hostings read, merge and write under the per-database Web Lock `tscache-persist:tscache` (§4.10.7 before 2026-10-31), the SharedWorker writing plainly and taking the lock from slice (b); changed after review round 2 to build the merged page with `hydrate` in a scratch state, with the lock requested under an `AbortSignal` at the writer's deadline (R1-2, R2-2), and at round 3 to keep removals per page (R3-1). Round 4 found two more issues in it (R4-1, R4-2). Director, 2026-10-30; redesign, 2026-10-30
