**Where the design lives.** A proposal file, `docs/design/m3-memory.md`, rather than a section of `docs/architecture.md`. The architecture document records approved contracts; this design has twenty-two open decision rows; one would amend an approved row of §4.10 (E11), and two would amend or qualify the M7 draft (E16, E21). Once the user decides them, the contract-test step moves the approved text into architecture §2.1, a new §2.9, §3.2, §4.3–§4.6, §4.9 and §4.10 and adds the decision files, as the persistence sketch became §4.10. Step 92 made the same choice for M7 and creates `docs/design/` on its own branch; this branch creates it too, so the two `docs/INDEX.md` rows for `docs/design/` merge into one row naming both files.

**What it covers,** as the brief asked: what is counted (segment bytes at allocated capacity, per dtype and presence mask) and what is not (coverage, warnings, read results, transient put and persistence buffers, object overhead), and why; the budget's scope (one per engine: per origin and worker URL under a SharedWorker, per tab otherwise, per `Engine` on a server) and its configuration (`ClientOptions.memoryBudget`, first value wins, and only on an engine that holds no cache yet); the eviction policy (least recently used pages, where a use is a read or a write of points by any tab); what is never evicted (a pending orchestrated `get`'s request and pages holding a provisional point; a call's own writes go last but are not pinned); the safety rule (an evicted page's whole slot range leaves coverage, so it reads as `uncached`, never as a confirmed gap); persistence (since the pre-review: an evicted page leaves the disk at the next flush, relaxed and never as a removal, so the disk and the eager load stay bounded; keeping it on disk needs lazy loading or a budgeted eager load, and a disk bound); the interactions with `cacheOnly`, orchestrated gets, `mergeWarning`, `restore`, `hydrate`, auth pending and `clearAll`; M7's change event; zero cost without a budget and the cost per operation with one; the public API, an additive protocol change and the version; and 21 contract and browser tests for the later step.

**Two findings the spec makes about earlier text.** (1) The first M7 draft (step 92) reported an eviction in `removed`, and its read rule makes every view showing a removed range refetch it. After an eviction that causes thrash across tabs whenever the views' ranges exceed the budget: each refetch evicts another view's pages. The proposal reports eviction in a separate `evicted` list that needs no re-read (E16), to be decided with M7; M7's current draft defers eviction reporting to M3's rows. (2) The §4.10.9 eviction row keeps an evicted page's record on disk; without a bound on the disk and on the eager load that is unbounded (the pre-review's P1-2), so the proposal recommends deleting it at the next flush until lazy loading and a disk budget exist (E11 (b)).

**Claims checked against the code.** Every statement about the current engine in the proposal's §2 table and §3 cites a file and line on `main` at `98b7bbf`; each was read at those lines, not inferred. The architecture and decision citations (§4.10.2 at line 1371, §4.10.6 property (a) at 1685, the §4.10.9 eviction row at 1777, §4.10.10 at 1781; N46, N57) were read in full.

## Decisions for the user

All twenty-two rows, E1–E22, are open and listed with options, pros, cons and a recommendation in §4 of the proposal. None is decided here; the proposal's §3 assumes each recommendation. Three rows need the user's explicit approval because they amend approved text or another proposal, if taken as recommended:

- **E11 (b)** (an evicted page's record is deleted at the next flush, relaxed, never as a removal) rewrites the §4.10.9 eviction row, which now says the record stays. Options (a) and (c) would instead amend §4.10.2 and §4.10.6 property (a), and (a) also N46 and N57.
- **E16 (a)** (an `evicted` list in `cacheChanged`) fills the eviction row of the M7 draft (§3.2 of `docs/design/m7-change-events.md`), which defers to M3, and is best decided together with M7's D2 and D12.
- **E21 (a)** (a removal over evicted coverage reports it) qualifies M7's D12 (a); decide it with D12.

**E22** (round 1; new behaviour if chosen as (b)): whether coverage-only pages, which count 0 bytes and keep small records stored and loaded eagerly, are bounded at all; recommended (a), not now.

One decision concern from the pre-review: **E7**, whether a `restore`'s pages count as recent (option (a), recommended) or enter the order at the least recent end (option (c)). The row now lists both, with their costs.
